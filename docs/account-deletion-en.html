Delete in the App

If you can sign in, go to Settings → Delete My Account and submit your request.

Submit a Web Request

If you cannot access the app, email us from the address registered to your account using the link below. Include your account email address in the request.

Send an Account Deletion Request

To protect your account, we may verify that the request belongs to you before processing it. We will never ask you to email your password or verification code.

What Happens Next

A 7-day waiting period applies, during which you may cancel the request. After that period, your user account, business workspaces you own, and their transaction, account, client, staff, product, note, document, and image data enter the deletion process.

Records you created as a team member in another business workspace may remain with that business to preserve its business records; the link to your deleted account is removed. Limited records may be retained only when required by law. Backup copies are removed through the ordinary backup lifecycle.
